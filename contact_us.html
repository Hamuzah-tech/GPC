Our Presence in Malawi

Headquarters

GPC main office is located in Lilongwe District, the capital city of Malawi. This is where our central coordination, administration, and strategic planning take place.

Operational Districts

In addition to Lilongwe, we actively implement programs in Zomba and Dowa Districts, reaching adolescent girls and young women in both urban and rural communities.

Community Reach

Through our sectoral programs, we partner with local leaders, schools, and health facilities to ensure AGYW across all three districts have access to empowerment opportunities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I volunteer with GPC?

You can volunteer by filling out our volunteer registration form on the Volunteer page. We welcome individuals with skills in mentoring, education, health, fundraising, and administration. After submitting your application, our team will contact you within 5-7 business days.

What types of partnerships does GPC engage in?

We partner with government agencies, NGOs, community-based organizations, private sector companies, and international donors. Partnerships focus on resource mobilization, technical expertise, joint program implementation, and advocacy campaigns.

How can I donate to support GPC's work?

You can donate via our secure online platform (coming soon) or contact us directly for bank transfer details. Donations support scholarships, health services, economic empowerment training, and climate action projects.

How does GPC select beneficiaries for its programs?

Beneficiaries are selected through community-based targeting, school referrals, and needs assessments. We prioritize marginalized adolescent girls and young women aged 14–25 in Lilongwe, Zomba, and Dowa Districts.

Can organizations request GPC to implement programs in their communities?

Yes! We welcome collaboration requests from CBOs, schools, faith-based organizations, and local government. Please reach out via email or the contact form with details about your community and needs.
