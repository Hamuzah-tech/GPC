About GPC

GPC is a women-led organization focused on addressing the diverse needs of Adolescent Girls and Young Women (AGYW) in Malawi. We create a supportive environment for their development through sectoral programs in Lilongwe, Zomba, and Dowa Districts.

Our mission is to empower AGYW through comprehensive programs that address gender equality, education, health, economic empowerment, climate action, and leadership development.
